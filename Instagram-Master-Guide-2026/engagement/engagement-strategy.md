# Engagement Strategy: Turning Reach into Relationships

Engagement is the "Gasoline" of the Instagram algorithm. High engagement signals to the AI that your content is valuable, which in turn increases your reach.

## The 2026 Engagement Framework

### 1. The Comment Strategy (Public Engagement)
- **First 60 Minutes:** Reply to every comment within the first hour of posting. This triggers a "velocity" signal.
- **The "Question" Method:** Always end your replies with a question to keep the conversation going.
- **The "Competitor" Method:** Engage with followers of similar accounts. Leave valuable comments (not spam) on their posts to build visibility.

### 2. The DM Strategy (Private Engagement)
In 2026, the DM is the most powerful place on Instagram.
- **Story Replies:** Encourage people to reply to your stories. Every DM exchange boosts your "Closeness" score.
- **Lead Magnets:** Use DMs to deliver value (e.g., "DM me 'PDF' for the guide").
- **Voice Notes:** Sending voice notes builds massive trust and humanizes your brand.

### 3. Broadcast Channels
Use Broadcast channels for:
- Behind-the-scenes polls.
- Early access to content.
- Personal updates that don't fit the main feed.
