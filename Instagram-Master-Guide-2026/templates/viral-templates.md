## DM & Outreach Templates

### The "Collaboration" Message
> "Hey [Name]! Love your latest Reel on [Topic]. Your point about [Specific Point] was spot on. I’m doing a series on [Your Topic] and think our audiences would love a Collab Post. Would you be open to a quick chat? 🚀"

### The "Lead Gen" Message
> "Hey [Name]! Thanks for the follow. I saw you’re interested in [Niche]. Are you currently working on [Goal], or just getting started? I have a free guide that might help if you’re interested! 🙌"

> **Subject:** Partnership Proposal: [Your Name] x [Brand Name] - Instagram Growth
>
> "Hi [Name/Marketing Team],
>
> I’ve been a long-time user of [Brand Name] and recently featured [specific product/feature] in my latest Reel which reached [Number] people in the [Niche] space.
>
> My audience of [Number] followers is highly engaged with [Topic], and I believe a partnership could drive significant [Awareness/Sales] for your upcoming [Campaign/Product].
>
> I’ve attached my media kit with current engagement rates (Avg. [Percentage]%) and would love to discuss how we can collaborate on a Reels series.
>
> Best,
> [Your Name]"
