# Community Building: Creating a "Cult" Following

Followers are a number; a community is an asset. To reach 1,000,000 followers and actually profit from them, you must build a community.

## The 3 Stages of Community
1. **The Audience:** People who watch your Reels occasionally.
2. **The Fans:** People who like and save your posts.
3. **The Super-Fans:** People who reply to every story, buy your products, and defend you in the comments.

## How to Build Community in 2026
- **Shared Vocabulary:** Create "Inside Jokes" or specific terms your community uses.
- **User-Generated Content (UGC):** Feature your followers' content or results on your page/stories.
- **Collaborative Storytelling:** Ask your audience to help you make decisions (e.g., "Which cover should I use?").
- **Consistency of Values:** Be clear about what you stand for.
