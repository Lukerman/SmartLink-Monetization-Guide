# Creator Branding in 2026

Branding is no longer just about a logo or a color palette. In 2026, branding is about **Pattern Recognition** and **Emotional Resonance**.

## The Branding Triangle
1. **Visual Consistency:** Using the same fonts, filters, and templates so that people recognize your content before they even see your username.
2. **Voice & Tone:** Are you the "No-BS expert," the "Friendly guide," or the "Relatable friend"?
3. **Values & Mission:** What do you stand for? Why should someone care about your growth?

## Core Branding Elements
- **Color Psychology:** Use colors that match your niche. (e.g., Blue for Tech/Trust, Green for Finance/Health, Neon for Gaming).
- **Typography:** Choose 2 fonts (1 for hooks, 1 for body) and stick to them across all Reels and Carousels.
- **Audio Branding:** Using consistent voiceover styles or a specific "vibe" of music.
