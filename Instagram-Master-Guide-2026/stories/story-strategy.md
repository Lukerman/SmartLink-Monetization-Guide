Stories are not for reach; they are for **Conversion**. This is where your true fans live.

## The "Daily Story" Rhythm
- **Morning:** Behind the scenes / Personal update (Humanize the brand).
- **Mid-day:** Value nugget / Quick tip / Q&A sticker (Engagement).
- **Evening:** CTA / Soft sell / Lifestyle (Monetization).

## 3 Story Frameworks for Growth

### 1. The "Bridge" Story
Share a Reel to your Story and add a "Spoiler" or "Additional Tip" to encourage people to click the Reel.
- **Technique:** Cover part of the Reel with a "Tap Here" sticker.

### 2. The Engagement Loop
Use Polls, Sliders, and Questions. The more someone interacts with your Story, the more likely they are to see your next Feed post.
- **The "Low Friction" Poll:** Ask an easy question (e.g., "Coffee or Tea?").

> [!TIP]

### 3. The DM Trigger
Ask a question like "Reply with 'YES' if you want the link." This opens a DM conversation, which is a massive positive signal to the algorithm.

## Story Ranking Factors
1. **Interaction History:** Likelihood of the user tapping, replying, or sharing.
2. **Closeness:** How often you DM that user.
3. **Interest:** How often you view that person's profile.
