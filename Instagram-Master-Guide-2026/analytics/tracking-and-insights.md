## Key Metrics to Track (Beyond Likes)

| Metric | Why it Matters | Goal |
|--------|----------------|------|
| **Follower Conversion Rate** | Reach divided by New Followers. | > 1-2% |
| **Sends per Reach** | The "Virality" metric. | > 1% for Reels |
| **Save Rate** | The "Value" metric. | > 0.5% |
| **Average Watch Time** | For Reels retention. | > 70% of video length |
| **Dwell Time** | For Carousels. | > 5s per slide |

## The Retention Graph Analysis
In 2026, Instagram shows you exactly where people stop watching.
- **The "Cliff":** A sharp drop at 0-3s. (Fix your Hook).
- **The "Slope":** A steady decline. (Fix your Pacing).
- **The "Spike":** People re-watching a specific part. (Double down on this content).

## How to Audit Your Account
1. **Monthly Review:** Identify your top 3 and bottom 3 posts.
2. **Pattern Analysis:** What did the top 3 have in common? (Hook style, Audio, Topic).
3. **Pivoting:** If a certain content style is consistently in the bottom 3, stop making it.

## The "Your Algorithm" Insight
In 2026, check which "Topics" Instagram has categorized your account into.
- **Check via:** Settings -> Content Preferences -> Topics.
