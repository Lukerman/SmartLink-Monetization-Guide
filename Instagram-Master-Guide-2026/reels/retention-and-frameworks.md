# 50 Retention Templates & 50 Viral Frameworks

## 50 Retention Templates (Keep them watching!)

### Visual Retention (1-15)
1. **The Progress Bar:** A thin line at the bottom showing time remaining.
2. **The "Wait for it" Loop:** A text overlay saying "Wait for the end" or "Shocking reveal in 3...".
3. **The Split Screen:** A satisfying video (sand cutting, power washing) on the bottom, your video on top.
4. **The Zoom-In/Zoom-Out:** Scaling the video up and down every 2-3 seconds.
5. **The Rapid-Fire Cut:** 5 clips shown in less than 2 seconds.
6. **The Dynamic Captions:** Words highlight or change color as you say them.
7. **The Perspective Shift:** Changing camera angles from front-facing to POV.
8. **The "Easter Egg":** Hiding a small object or text and asking users to find it.
9. **The Satisfying Transition:** Using a "wipe," "blur," or "mask" to change scenes.
10. **The Pattern Interrupt:** A sudden black-and-white frame or a slow-motion clip.
11. **The On-Screen Timer:** Counting down to a "Big Reveal."
12. **The Ghost Effect:** Using overlays to show a "future" version of yourself.
13. **The Prop Hook:** Holding a physical object relevant to the niche (e.g., a phone, a book).
14. **The Green Screen:** Reacting to a viral tweet or news article behind you.
15. **The Blur Reveal:** Starting the video blurry and focusing slowly.

### Audio & Psychological Retention (16-35)
16. **The Beat Drop Sync:** Timing a transition perfectly with a bass drop.
17. **The Silence Gap:** Cutting all audio for 1 second to create tension.
18. **The "Whisper" Hook:** Starting very quiet so the user turns up their volume.
19. **The Sound Effect (SFX):** Using "Woosh," "Ding," and "Pop" sounds for text.
20. **The Question Loop:** Starting with "Have you ever wondered why...?"
21. **The Cliffhanger:** Saying "But there's one thing you MUST avoid..." and pausing.
22. **The Social Proof Pop-up:** Showing screenshots of results/comments during the talk.
23. **The Story Bridge:** "Last week I showed you X, but today we're doing Y."
24. **The Contrarian Statement:** "Why I hate [Popular Tool] (and what I use instead)."
25. **The Challenge:** "Try to watch this without [Action]."
26. **The Reward Loop:** "Stick around for the free template at the end."
27. **The Expert Bias:** "After 5 years in [Niche], here's what I learned."
28. **The "Secret" Signal:** Using emojis on screen to categorize tips.
29. **The Multi-Character Skit:** You playing two different people talking.
30. **The Handwriting Effect:** Showing text being "written" on screen in real-time.
31. **The Screen Recording:** Showing a live tutorial on your phone/computer.
32. **The "Mistake" Reveal:** "I made a massive mistake so you don't have to."
33. **The Comparison:** "Old Way vs. New AI Way."
34. **The Urgency:** "This tool will be paid next week, get it now."
35. **The Personal Vulnerability:** "I've never shared this before, but..."

### Technical & Format Retention (36-50)
36. **The Perfect Loop:** Ending the video where it started for infinite views.
37. **The "Read the Caption" Pause:** Showing a very fast list and telling them to "Pause to read."
38. **The Multiple Text Boxes:** Spreading information across the screen so the eye moves.
39. **The Emoji Reaction:** Using the "floating emoji" effect during key moments.
40. **The Subtitles only:** No talking, just text on screen with music.
41. **The ASMR Hook:** High-quality sound of a physical action.
42. **The Day in the Life:** 10 clips of your day in 15 seconds.
43. **The Checklist Overlay:** Checking off items as you mention them.
44. **The Floating Head:** Circular crop of your face over a screen recording.
45. **The Map/Diagram:** Visualizing a concept with a simple drawing.
46. **The AI Avatar:** Using a digital twin to deliver the message.
47. **The Kinetic Typography:** Text that moves and animates to the rhythm.
48. **The Depth of Field:** Using a blurry background to make the subject pop.
49. **The Color Grade Shift:** Changing the mood from "Sad/Dark" to "Bright/Happy."
50. **The Final Summary:** A 1-second flash of all tips for people to screenshot.

## 50 Viral Reel Frameworks

2. **The "I was today years old":** Finding a solution to a problem you didn't know you had.

4. **The Step-by-Step:** A 3-step guide to solving [Pain Point].
