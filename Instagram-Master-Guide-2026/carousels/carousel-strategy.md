# Carousel Strategy: Driving Dwell Time & Saves

In 2026, Carousels are the best way to build **Authority** and drive **Saves**. They allow for deep-dive educational content that Reels can't match.

## The Viral Carousel Structure (10 Slides)

- **Slide 1: The Cover.** A bold hook and a high-contrast image. Must look clickable in Explore.
- **Slide 2: The Hook Reinforcement.** Explain why this topic matters *now*.
- **Slide 3-8: The Value.** 1 key point per slide. Use minimal text and high-quality visuals.
- **Slide 9: The Summary.** A "Cheatsheet" slide that is highly saveable.
- **Slide 10: The CTA.** A clear instruction to follow, share, or comment.

## Why Carousels Work in 2026
1. **Dwell Time:** The more slides someone swipes through, the more "Dwell Time" you get.
2. **Double Exposure:** If someone scrolls past your Carousel in the feed, Instagram will show it to them again later, but starting from **Slide 2**.
3. **SEO Weight:** You can include more text and keywords in Carousel slides, which helps with search indexing.
