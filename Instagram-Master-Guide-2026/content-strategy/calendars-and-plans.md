# Content Calendars & Growth Plans

Successful creators don't guess what to post; they plan for it.

## The 30-Day "Kickstart" Calendar (0-1k Followers)
- **Goal:** Find your "Viral Signal."
- **Schedule:** 1 Reel/day + 3 Stories/day.
- **Focus:** Broad hooks and trending audio in your niche.

## The 90-Day "Authority" Calendar (1k-50k Followers)
- **Goal:** Build a loyal community.
- **Schedule:** 5 Reels/week + 2 Carousels/week + Daily Stories.
- **Focus:** Mixing "Viral Reach" Reels with "High-Value" Carousels.

## The 365-Day "Millionaire" Plan (50k-1M+ Followers)
- **Phase 1 (Month 1-3):** Testing 3 different content styles.
- **Phase 2 (Month 4-6):** Doubling down on the style with the highest "Share" rate.
- **Phase 3 (Month 7-9):** Implementing AI automation for high-volume posting (2+ Reels/day).
- **Phase 4 (Month 10-12):** Cross-platform expansion and massive monetization.
